What the scrim is actually doing

A photograph behind white text is the classic way this design fails, because the moment you change the image the text becomes unreadable somewhere. The scrim solves it structurally rather than per-photo. It is heaviest at the very top where the clock and the speed live, lightest across the middle band where nothing is drawn, then lifts again at the bottom so the hero meets the dock cleanly. Whatever photograph you put behind it, the text regions stay dark. The small text shadows are a second layer of insurance for bright images.

WORTH BUILDINGAutomatic night dimming

While digging into the handbrake problem I found a second vendor signal sitting next to it in the framework: vehicle_signal_ill_detect. That is illumination detect, which is the head unit reading your headlight switch. It is the same mechanism the unit already uses to dim its own screen at night.

If that signal is wired on your car, and it is far more commonly connected than the parking wire because it drives the existing auto-dim, the launcher can read it and move the scrim from Standard to Night the moment you switch your headlights on. Wallpaper visible during the day, near-black at night, no setting for you to remember. If the signal turns out to be unwired like the handbrake one, we fall back to sunrise and sunset times calculated from the GPS position we already have.

Wallpaper, practically

ConcernAnswer
Memory1280 x 720 decoded as RGB_565 is about 1.8 MB. Decoded once at startup, held for the life of the launcher. On 1.92 GB this is not worth thinking about.
Source image sizeAnything larger than 1280 x 720 gets downsampled at decode with inSampleSize, so a 12 megapixel phone photo costs the same as a correctly sized one.
Aspect ratioCentre-cropped. A portrait photo will lose its top and bottom. Landscape images work best, unsurprisingly.
Changing itThe unit's own wallpaper setting. The launcher re-reads on resume, so it updates when you come back to the home screen.
No wallpaper setFalls back to flat black, which is the Direction B you already approved. Nothing breaks.
